# Repair Records

> Append-only. What was DONE about a REJECT, recorded beside the verdict rather than
> replacing it: what the reviewer found stays true, and the disposition becomes visible.
> A repair closing fewer findings than the verdict raised is PARTIAL and names the rest.
> A finding closed by FILING an artefact is recorded as filed, with the id - 'fixed' and
> 'filed as a known issue' are both legitimate, and telling them apart afterwards is not
> optional.
