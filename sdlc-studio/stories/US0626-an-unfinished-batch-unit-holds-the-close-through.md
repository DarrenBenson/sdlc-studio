## User Story

**As a** operator closing a sprint
**I want** an unfinished batch unit to hold the close through its stop-ship step (D0193), naming the unit and where its findings went, while units at Review, Fixed or their rung's end do not
**So that** a run cannot be recorded as finished over work that never reached a terminal status

## Acceptance Criteria

The shared predicate is `sprint.unanswered_units(root, state, retro_id=None)`. It returns `{"unanswered": [{"unit", "status", "why", "filed"}], "rulings_from": <retro id or None>}`, and every criterion below asserts on it or on a reader of it. A unit is ANSWERED by status when it is delivered-terminal (`sdlc_md.is_delivered_terminal`), abandoned (terminal and not delivered-terminal - derived, never listed), at Review awaiting nothing but a signature, at its run's rung-end status (`RUNG_TERMINALS[run_rung(state)]`, never on the `done` rung), dropped from `batch`, parked on a pending decision or depending on one through declared `Depends on:` edges, or ruled `not-stop-ship`, `accepted-risk` or `deferred` in the carried table. A Review unit awaits nothing but a signature exactly when `sprint._awaits_signoff` returns True, and the predicate calls it rather than restating it: its adversarial pass is recorded (a `critic.evidence_for` row, or sprint-level coverage via `critic.sprint_covers_independently` over `critic.sprint_review_for`), AND the two-role bar applies to it (a `review.two_role_after` cutoff is set and the unit's id is past it), AND no independent sign-off is recorded yet (`critic.is_independent_signoff` over `critic.signoff_for`). Every other Review unit is UNANSWERED: its `why` names `adversarial pass owed` when neither evidence limb holds - D0193's "awaiting sign-off" means only the signature is outstanding, and the pass is work this session can still dispatch (BG0455) - and otherwise `remaining work at Review`, which covers (a) no `review.two_role_after` set, (b) the unit at or below the cutoff and (c) its sign-off already independent, so a Done transition is all it lacks. `stop` keeps its HEAD refusal on all three and names each on it (AC9). The close reads the predicate BEFORE apply-signoff moves any unit (AC10), so a (c) unit is answered at the close only once it already stands at Done. The predicate walks the live `batch` and every id a `batch_changes` drop removed that the live batch no longer holds. A unit whose standing delivery verdict is a REJECT is UNANSWERED at every non-abandoned status and whatever else answers it - recorded evidence, a drop from `batch`, a park and a ruling included - unless `critic.coverage_state` reads `approved` or `repaired` (D0196c; a unit with no verdict is not a REJECT case). The carried table read is the named retro's when the caller passes one (the close's `--retro`), otherwise the latest retro, by id, whose text carries the run's recorded `run_id`; when no retro does, a ruling is unreadable and answers nothing.

### AC10: the close reads the predicate before apply-signoff, so an already-signed Review unit is answered only once it stands at Done

The close sequence at HEAD runs the other way round from "apply-signoff carries it to Done before the checklist reads": `cmd_close` runs every `_CLOSE_CHAIN` step, `checklist` sixth, in its loop at sprint.py:9221-9232, and calls `_apply_signoff` only after the loop completes, at sprint.py:9257-9261, on every invocation, `--apply-signoff` included. So apply-signoff cannot answer shape (c) at the close. What answers it is a Done transition made before the close's checklist step: `transition.py`, which the two-role gate admits because both halves are recorded. On the same state the close and stop give the same answer.

- **Given** AC2's done-rung harness - `review.two_role_after: 100`, every compulsory checklist row waived, known-issues included, RETRO0001 carrying the run's id - whose batch holds US0101 with a `critic.evidence_for` row and an independent sign-off recorded through `critic.record_signoff`, in two trees: (i) US0101 left at Review; (ii) US0101 moved to Done through `transition.transition(root, "US0101", "Done")` before the close runs
- **When** `sprint.py close --retro RETRO0001 --apply-signoff --principal Darren` runs in (i), and `sprint.py close --retro RETRO0001` in (ii), each with every chain step patched EXCEPT `checklist`; and `unanswered_units` is read and `sprint.py stop` runs in (i) before the close
- **Then** in (i) the close stops at the checklist step with its `known-issues:` line naming `US0101 (Review)` and `remaining work at Review`, prints no `apply-signoff:` line, and US0101 still reads Review afterwards - the order pinned: the checklist refused before apply-signoff ran; stop refuses naming US0101 and `unanswered` holds it; in (ii) the checklist step passes, the close completes and US0101 is absent from `unanswered`. The consequence the builder must keep: a `close --apply-signoff` re-run after a stop between a unit's sign-off and its Done transition refuses at the checklist naming that unit, and its Done transition is the remedy
- **Mutant:** treat an independent sign-off as the answer because apply-signoff will carry the unit - the close passes a unit it has not yet moved, while stop refuses the same state
