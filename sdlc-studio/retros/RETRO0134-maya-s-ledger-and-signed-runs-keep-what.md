## Keep

- Reviewers made their own `git worktree add --detach` checkout instead of the harness's isolation, so each REJECT's reviewer stayed resumable for its next round (BG1003, US1006, US1004), where the harness's worktree had stranded BG0950's.
- `critic.py record --ruling` (BG1003, BG1044) put the operator's past-cap decisions on the ledger: BG0993's rounds 3 and 4 under D0352 and D0353, and US1004's full-depth round under D0368.

## Stop

- Writing a claim into a changelog or commit message before a test can fail on it: US1004's "cannot drift", BG1003's "seven mutants" left unnamed, BG1045's "was caught before" and BG1044's M13 were each over-claims a reviewer had to find (LC-018).
- Widening a unit's Affects in a repair without re-deriving its review tier: US1004 was approved twice at the light tier its band no longer met, and the gap surfaced only at the close (BG1052).
- Fixture dates fixed against a real clock: test_status's 2026-07-16 bugs aged into stale on day 90 and turned every tree red (BG1047).

## Try

- Re-derive the review tier at every brief and record, and name an under-depth APPROVE the moment it exists (BG1052).
- Before a review, put each changelog claim through the shipped entry point against its strongest negative, and name every mutant a message counts.
- Date a fixture from today whenever the code under test reads the real clock, and pin the boundary day as BG1047's reviewer did.
