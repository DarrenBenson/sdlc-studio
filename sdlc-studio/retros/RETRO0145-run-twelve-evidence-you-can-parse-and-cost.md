- Stop grooming a unit that adds a script, a verb or reference text without the registry files it implies: five lands were refused for a generated page, a mechanics-verb entry, a catalogue heading, a reading guide or a line ceiling that the unit's Affects did not name.

- Stop writing placeholder ids into a finding's fields file: file_finding kept changelog.d/BGXXXX.md verbatim in seventeen bugs, and BG1441's self-check was refused for it.
