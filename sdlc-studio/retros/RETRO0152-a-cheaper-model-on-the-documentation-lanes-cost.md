> **Run:** RUN-01M7TTK3
> **Batch:** BG1096, BG1162, BG1168, BG1180, BG1516, BG1517, BG1525, BG1526, BG1527, BG1528, BG1529, BG1530, BG1531, BG1533, BG1543, BG1546, BG1547, BG1553, BG1554, BG1555, BG1556, BG1557, BG1565, BG1621, BG1631, BG1632, BG1633, BG1634, BG1644, BG1647, US1296, US1297, US1298, US1299

## Keep

- Folding a unit's repair into the next builder that works the same file. The wave-two TRD lane carried the BG1516, BG1517 and BG1531 repairs beside five new TRD units (BG1096, BG1556, BG1557, BG1565, BG1621), so eight units landed in one commit (6aee7a42) through one hook run, and all three repairs passed their second round.
- Repairing on the default model against a brief that quotes the REJECT verbatim. Every repair this run passed its second round: BG1180, BG1525, BG1543, BG1553, BG1516, BG1517, BG1531 and US1299.
- The push gate's full parallel suite on the gate host. It refused two pushes on defects no per-commit lane could see: two tests that passed alone and failed beside others (BG1644, BG1647), and a PRD change with no Changelog row (US1299). All three were fixed before origin moved, and the third push went green on 608 modules.

## Stop

- Stop running documentation lanes on the sonnet tier (D0549's trial). Six of its sixteen units were rejected in round one, and every self-check it recorded needed repairing, so the cheaper build bought a repair round and a second review for more than a third of its units.
- Stop starting a fresh reviewer for round two. `critic.py record` accepts round two only from the reviewer who rejected round one, so two fresh reviewers' work was refused and thrown away (BG1641).
- Stop starting reviewers while a main-tree commit's hooks run. A verdict file written mid-commit tripped the repo-writes lane and refused the paperwork commit once.
- Stop briefing a builder before its unit's Affects is groomed in full. BG1634 had no changelog fragment and US1299 lacked tsd.md, and each needed a widening and a second hand-in.

## Try

- Before briefing any builder, commit the grooming, with every criterion carrying a Verify line. US1299's AC3 had none and blocked Done after two approved rounds (D0551).
- A builder that edits a `reference-*.md` page runs `docgen.py reading-guides` and `tools/lint-style.sh` before hand-in. The budgets and style lanes each refused a landing on a check the builder could have run.
- Route every round two to the reviewer who rejected round one: resume that reviewer by SendMessage with a fresh `critic.py brief`.
