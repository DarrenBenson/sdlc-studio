## Summary

RFC0063's WS3 items for 6.4. EP0305 made Abena Osei, a head of engineering in an audited organisation, the shipped skill's Primary, but the front door still reads as it did for a solo founder. README.md runs to 5,238 words with no route for someone evaluating, trying or upgrading. SUPPORT.md does not say plainly what support exists. No page shows the evidence a change board reads: the signed sprint reports, partial verdicts, and how long a run waits for its signature. No page tells an engineering leader how change approval, separation of duties, the signed report and cost work.

## Impact

An engineering leader evaluating the skill cannot find, in one sitting, what it guarantees, what it costs, or what evidence it leaves for a change board. They must read a 5,000-word README written for a different reader.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] README.md is about 1,500 words and opens with three routes in (evaluate, try, upgrade), each leading to a page that serves it. Fails on: a README over 1,800 words, or a route that leads nowhere.
- [ ] SUPPORT.md states what support exists, where to ask, and what is not supported, and it matches the repository's current channels. Fails on: a channel named that does not exist.
- [ ] An evidence page is generated from the committed signed reports. It shows each run's verdict, partial verdicts with their reasons, cost per point, and the time each run waited for its signature, and it is regenerated by a command, not edited by hand. Fails on: a figure on the page that the reports do not hold.
- [ ] A page for engineering leaders explains change approval, separation of duties (author, reviewer and signer), the signed report, and cost in dollars a point, each with a link to the mechanism that enforces it. Fails on: a claim with no linked mechanism.
