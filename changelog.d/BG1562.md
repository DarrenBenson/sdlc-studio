`archive.py archive` now refuses a `--release` label that is not one plain path segment: a label
with a path separator, an absolute or drive-lettered form, a bare `.` or `..`, an empty label or one
holding a NUL byte exits non-zero naming the label, and writes nothing and leaves the live index as
it was. Before, a label such as `../../x` wrote the archive sub-index outside the workspace and
removed the rows from the live table. A plain label such as `r2.5` archives as before.
