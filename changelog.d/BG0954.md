`transition.py set --status Done` now refuses a Draft or Proposed story that misses the
Definition of Ready `sprint plan` applies, naming each unmet item: no `Affects`, an `Affects`
whose every path is a typo for a file that exists elsewhere, or no `Points` on the scale. Before,
a story could go from Draft to Done on green criteria alone, so one closed outside a sprint never
met the readiness a planned story is refused without. `transition.py requirements` lists the same
items. A recorded `> **Decision-Override:** <reason>` on the story lets the move through and the
reason is reported, `sprint.breakdown: judgement` reports the items instead of refusing, and
`--force` waives the check with the override recorded on the story. A story already past Ready
is not asked again, and Won't Implement, Deferred and Superseded stay reachable from Draft.
