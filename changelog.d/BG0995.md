`refine.py apply` no longer seeds a duplicated label from a request whose criteria are written
`**AC1** text` or `**AC1:** text`, the rows the skill's own filers write: the seeded heading reads
`### AC1: text`. A label closed in bold now strips with a separator inside the bold, after it, or
none; a plain label still needs a separator, as before. A criterion that opens on a bold phrase,
such as `**AC1 is met** when`, keeps its text.
