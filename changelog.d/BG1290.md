- `sprint lane accept` no longer records a verdict against an earlier round's QA brief (BG1290).
  A landing that wrote no brief, because its self-check was refused or not handed in, left the
  earlier round's `sdlc-studio/.local/briefs/<unit>-qa.txt` in place, and `lane accept` recorded
  an APPROVE against it and moved the unchecked repair to Done. `sprint lane land` now removes
  each unit's brief before it lands, so a brief found afterwards is one that landing wrote. A
  unit left with no brief gets `<unit>-qa.none` beside it, naming the landing and why it wrote
  none. `lane accept` then refuses, records nothing and quotes that note.
