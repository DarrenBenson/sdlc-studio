- `critic.py record --ruling DNNNN` records a review round past `review.max_rounds` that an
  operator authorised. Before, nothing could, and the ledger's last word on the unit stayed a
  REJECT the unit had moved past.
  - **What it requires.** The decision must be accepted in `sdlc-studio/decisions.md`, name
    the unit as its subject (the first work-item id in its text), and not have authorised a
    round on that unit already: one ruling, one round. The round still comes from the
    reviewer who rejected.
  - **How it's recorded.** The ruling is recorded beside the row it authorised, and readers
    annotate that round with it.
  - **What it refuses.** A missing, unaccepted or unrelated decision is refused by name, as
    a round past the cap always was.
