`critic.py brief` now warns on stderr when the unit's Affects hold uncommitted changes, naming
each path (modified, staged, deleted or untracked, a declared directory's files included) and
saying an isolated reviewer will not see them: a reviewer's worktree is made from HEAD and judges
the unit without them. A clean tree prints no warning, the review tooling's own writes are not
named, and git unable to give a status is said rather than read as a clean tree. The brief also
records the commit it was cut at, in its note beside the fingerprint and on a `brief cut at
commit:` line beneath the fingerprint line, so a verdict recorded against that fingerprint can be
matched to the revision it judged. The re-review brief does both. Outside a repository neither
the warning nor a commit is given.
