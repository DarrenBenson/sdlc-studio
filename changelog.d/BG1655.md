`validate.py seats` no longer warns `seat-persona-unknown` over ordinary prose on a seat card.
A question or auxiliary word (`the Primary persona, Does it hold?`), an acronym (`Serve the CLI
(Primary)`) or the word opening a clause after a colon (`the Primary persona: When they
arrive`) is not read as a name unless the cast holds it, while a capitalised name the cast
lacks still warns. A first name followed by another surname no longer passes as the cast
member: where the cast holds only Maya Okafor, `the Primary persona is Maya Smith` now warns,
and `Maya` and `Maya Okafor` still resolve. A persona that `personas/index.md` omits is now
checked against their card's Cast role, so a seat card calling them another role warns
`seat-persona-role`; before, the role check was skipped.
