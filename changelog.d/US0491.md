### Added

- `sprint call` finishes a run at a point rather than abandoning it: what was delivered is
  judged against the Sprint Goal and the close paperwork runs, while the units nobody started
  leave the batch. The remainder returns to the **backlog**, never forward to the next charter,
  so no two sprints are coupled and the next run never inherits a batch it did not approve. Each
  descoped unit keeps its own status, because the drop judges the batch and not the work. A
  descope needs a reason, matching what `batch drop` already requires.
