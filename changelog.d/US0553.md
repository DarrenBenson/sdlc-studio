- **The close records the gate verdict it earned (US0553).** `sprint close` runs the FULL suites as step 4 of seven and then commits; it recorded that green only in its own close-private ledger, so the commit hook - which reads `gate-suite-verdict.json` - knew nothing about it and the first commit re-earned a verdict paid for seconds earlier. Nothing new decides anything here: `suite_decision` has known how to reuse a green since US0493. What was missing was a caller. A refused gate records nothing, so one red close cannot buy a green for the commit after it, and a close that reused a verdict does not re-stamp it as a fresh measurement.
