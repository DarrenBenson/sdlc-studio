`critic.py self-check record` accepts a claim ruled `TRUE: <evidence>` (or `FALSE:` or
`UNVERIFIABLE:`) and records it as that ruling, the evidence kept with the claim and named
when a FALSE ruling is refused. A ruling with anything after the word and no colon, such as
`TRUE in substance`, is still refused. `critic.py record --dry-run` checks a verdict as
`record` would, every refusal included, says what it would record and writes nothing. A
finding that puts an asterisk between two code spans now writes a ledger row markdownlint
passes.
