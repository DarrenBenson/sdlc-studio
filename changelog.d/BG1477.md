`sprint.py lane accept --agent <id>` no longer charges a lane's whole transcript to each unit it
built. Where `lane land` already split that transcript across the lane's units by points, the
accept keeps each unit's share and says so, so the units' tokens still sum to the transcript
once. Before, the accept's `lane return` repriced each unit at the whole transcript, doubling a
two-unit lane's cost. A unit the land did not price is still priced at its transcript in full.
