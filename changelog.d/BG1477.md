`sprint.py lane accept --agent <id>` no longer charges a lane's whole transcript to each unit it
built. Where that transcript was already split across the lane's units by points, by `lane land`
or by an earlier `lane return` of several units, the accept keeps each unit's share and says so,
so the units' tokens still sum to the transcript once. Before, the accept's `lane return`
repriced each unit at the whole transcript, doubling a two-unit lane's cost. The `--minutes` and
`--model` the accept supplies are recorded on the kept share and named in its output; what it
does not supply stays as the share holds it. An accept giving `--tokens` records those tokens,
an accept naming another agent prices the unit from that agent's own transcript, and a
transcript that has grown since it was split is warned about with the share kept. A unit the
land did not price is still priced at its transcript in full.
