- **A bug-heavy sprint had no route to a close (sign-off fan-out).** `_apply_signoff` selected
  `hit[1] == "story"`, so every bug in a batch was skipped and reported "NOT reached by the
  fan-out". Meanwhile `sprint close` refuses at step 1 of 10 on units no independent pass covers,
  and the two-role gate applies to any numbered id past the cutoff - `conformance.two_role_applies_to`
  fails closed. So a 41-bug programme could be fully delivered and still could not close, and the
  only mechanism for covering those units skipped all of them.
  The fan-out now reaches bugs. Each unit is carried to its OWN terminal - `Fixed` for a bug,
  `Done` for a story - through `transition.transition`, so a bug meets the verification-depth
  floor and the planned-mutant check its type demands rather than reaching a terminal status by
  a Done-shaped path that skips them. `_batch_unfanned_units` no longer lists what the fan-out
  reaches, preserving the invariant that every batch unit is either fanned into or reported
  outstanding, never both.
  Three tests changed with it, and they were pinning the defect: two asserted that a bug is
  reported "NOT reached", and one asserted the sum of fanned and outstanding units over a batch
  where bugs now appear in the first list rather than the second. Applying the fix as a mutant
  is what surfaced them (LL0051).
