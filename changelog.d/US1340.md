The seats section of `reference-workflow-personas.md` now says what a seat may change in the
document it reviews against. Its delegated authority lets it correct a fact itself: a feature's
status, a criterion verified work has met, a count, a date or a link. The page links to the
review's auto-apply step (step 3b of `reference-review.md`), which makes the first three without
confirmation. A change to what the document requires (a requirement added, removed or reworded, a
priority or the scope) the seat proposes as a change request carrying the wording, and the person
decides it: a seat never writes the requirements it signs against. A seat unsure which kind a
change is treats it as a proposal and asks the person. `prd review`, which the person runs, still
adds newly found features to the PRD directly.
