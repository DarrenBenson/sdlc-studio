A persona card's End goals are read correctly in three more shapes. A `<!--` inside a fenced
block or an inline code span no longer opens a comment that hides every goal below it. A nested
`Verify` or `Applies-to` line under an unindented paragraph that follows a blank line, such as a
`Notes:` paragraph, attaches to no goal instead of the one above. A goal's second `Verify` line
is no longer silently taken in place of the first: the first stands and `persona-goal-verify`
warns of the second, naming the card and the goal.
