`tools/lint_corpus.py` now reports an RFC whose Status is Accepted while its Decision outcome
still reads pending, under the rule `RFC-DECISION`, attributed against the baseline like a
markdownlint finding. An RFC not yet Accepted, one with a filled outcome and one with no Outcome
line are not reported. RFC0063 and RFC0064, accepted under D0474 with the Decision section
unfilled, now state their outcome, date, rationale, the decisions that resolved each open
question and the CRs they spawned. RFC0063's workstream rows that promised a new CR no unit
carried (WS1 to WS3, WS5 to WS7) now name the CR that carries them or the decision that defers
them.
