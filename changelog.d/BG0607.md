- A unit's standing verdict is no longer whichever row was written last. A panel is several seats recorded one after another, so a unit REJECTed by one and APPROVEd by another reported whatever the recorder happened to write second - the verdict was a fact about invocation order. An unanswered REJECT now stands until a repair answers it, with one boundary: a seat may retire its OWN rejection, because that is a re-review of the same work, while another seat's approval cannot speak for it.
