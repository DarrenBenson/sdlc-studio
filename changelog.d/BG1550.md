`docs/engineering-leaders.md` and the `evidence_pack.py` entry in `reference-scripts-domain.md`
no longer say the evidence pack refuses to name an agent as the accountable person. Both now
state the three signer refusals the pack enforces: a blank principal, one that reads as an agent
by name (the word agent or subagent in it), and one the committed review ledgers record as an
author of the report's own units, unless that author is recorded as a person. Both say the pack
cannot tell a person from an agent session signing under another name, so the person it names is
the operator's responsibility. Two tests in `tools/tests/test_engineering_leaders_page.py` refuse
an unqualified agent refusal on either page and fail when the two pages disagree on the refusals
or the limit.
