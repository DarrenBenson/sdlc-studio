## Why flags are not here

There are 286 option strings across this surface. A table of them would need a line-budget
allowlist entry on the day it was born, and a page nobody can afford to keep goes stale rather
than helping. Two things answer the flag question properly and neither can drift: the script's
own `--help`, and `docgen.py surface --format json`.
