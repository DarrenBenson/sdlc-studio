# Sprint Charter Queue

The runs that are planned but have not happened. A charter carries the shape of a run - its
goal, the rule that selects its batch, and its appetite - and `sprint next` materialises the
head of this queue against the backlog **as it stands at that moment**, never against the
backlog as it stood when the charter was written.
